# Terminology glossary — corrections the notes generator applies

Speech-to-text reliably mangles names, acronyms, and jargon. List the correct
forms here and the local model will fix them in your meeting notes without being
asked. Edit this to match the vocabulary of your meetings.

Examples (replace with your own):

- ACME — Association for Computing Machinery Examples (NOT "ackme", "akmey")
- Jane Okafor — a frequent attendee (NOT "Jane O'Connor", "Jane Okofor")
- Q3 OKRs — quarterly objectives (NOT "cue three okers")

If you encounter an unfamiliar acronym not listed here, keep it as heard and add
"(?)" so a human can verify — do not guess its meaning.
